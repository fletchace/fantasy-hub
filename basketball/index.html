How Lock-In works

Matchups run Monday through Sunday.
A player has to be in your lineup before tip-off for that game to count.
After his game ends, you can lock it any time before his next game starts. Locked games can't be undone.
Once he's locked, his later games that week don't count.
Never lock him and his team's last game of the week counts automatically, even if he sits.
Points only count for games played while he was on your roster.
